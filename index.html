INFORMATION & SAFETY

Safety Guide

General information for using online resources and digital tools with greater awareness of privacy risks.

Your device and privacy

  • If you believe a device may be monitored, use a device you trust when seeking sensitive information, if one is safely available.
  • Be aware that browser history, notifications, shared accounts, location settings, and saved passwords may reveal activity.
  • Private browsing does not make activity invisible to device administrators, network operators, or monitoring software.
  • Consider whether downloaded files, messages, or printed materials could be seen by someone else.

Using resource listings

  • Confirm an organization's services and eligibility directly before relying on a listing.
  • Avoid sharing more personal information than necessary when contacting an organization.
  • Ask about confidentiality, recordkeeping, and communication preferences before providing sensitive details.
  • A listing is informational and is not an endorsement or guarantee of service availability.

Limits of digital protection

Encryption and local storage can reduce certain risks, but they cannot guarantee protection against a compromised device, monitoring software, unsafe backups, or access to an unlocked computer or phone.

Survivors Network does not promise that any device, website, communication channel, or storage method is completely secure.

Immediate danger

If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services when it is safe to do so. If calling is unsafe, consider whether a trusted person or a local support organization can help identify a safer way to reach assistance.

This guide provides general information, not individualized safety planning, legal advice, or emergency response. Your circumstances and safest options may differ.
